**Short version:** this website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or
tracking, and has no forms or comments. I don't collect any information
about you.

### Who is responsible
Matteo Totaro, owner of this personal website. You can reach me at the
e-mail address linked on the home page.

### What happens when you visit
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (USA). As with any web
  server, Netlify processes your IP address and basic request data
  (page requested, browser type, time) to deliver the pages and protect
  the service, and keeps them in its server logs for a limited time.
  I don't use these logs to identify visitors. Legal basis: legitimate
  interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  See [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).
- **Photos.** Some older photo galleries are served by Cloudinary Ltd.,
  which therefore receives your IP address when it delivers the images.
  See [Cloudinary's privacy policy](https://cloudinary.com/privacy).
- **No cookies, no local storage for tracking.** The site only stores your
  light/dark theme choice in your browser, which never leaves your device.

- **Objectify demo.** The object-detection demo runs entirely in your
  browser: camera images never leave your device. Only if you press
  "Start Camera", the detection model files are downloaded from Google's
  TensorFlow storage (storage.googleapis.com), which receives your IP
  address. They are then kept in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB)
  so the demo starts faster next time; you can delete them by clearing
  this site's data in your browser.

### External links
Links to Spotify, YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, LinkedIn and others only
open those sites when you click them. From then on, their own privacy
policies apply.

### Your rights
Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of
personal data, and you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data
protection authority ([Garante per la protezione dei dati
personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/)).
